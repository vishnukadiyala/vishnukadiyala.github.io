Publication

Needing Partner Information Is Not Benefiting from Predicting It: When Teammate-Prediction Training Helps Cooperative Agents

Vishnu P. Kadiyala, Mohammed Atiquzzaman

Under review at ICLR 2027. The abstract and PDF will be added when a public version is posted.

Summary

Cooperative agents are often trained to predict their teammates as an auxiliary task. This paper shows that an agent needing information about its partner does not imply that it benefits from being trained to predict the partner, and it identifies when such training helps.
